# Three-class lab revision 2 — November 4, 2026

This revision improves the quality of personal training/validation and makes the Thursday lab session easier to recover after a restart. The binary application, its ZIP and the historical participant08 three-class reference weights are preserved. No new live-wearer accuracy is claimed.

## Changes that affect the lab workflow

- **Cue labels follow display confirmation.** The browser paints each cue before sending a matching run/trial acknowledgement; its receipt starts that cue's server clock. Transition intervals are unlabeled. A duplicate cannot reset timing, stale acknowledgements are refused, and missing acknowledgements abort the run. Physical display latency still needs a measured marker path.
- **Later blocks tune the model.** Calibration uses randomized balanced three-cue blocks. The final quarter of the original blocks is reserved before signal-quality filtering, while earlier whole blocks train the network. Default clean data gives nine training and three tuning cues per class. Bad later tuning cues cannot move into training. Normalization/covariance references still use training only.
- **Training selection matches the decision rule.** Raw average probabilities can look correct while confidence/margin rules reject every update. Tuning now records raw cue accuracy AND the deployed decision-policy estimate, confidence coverage, rejected cues, per-class recalls and train/tuning gaps. Early stopping uses the decision-policy estimate, then worst recall and raw cue loss. No fresh validation data select settings. The calibration estimate covers clean extracted windows and is explicitly separate from continuous prospective validation.
- **Saved models can be loaded explicitly.** A paused console can list and load an earlier personal model after checking participant ID, montage/order, preprocessing, hash and disjoint calibration cue provenance. Versions sort newest first rather than best score. Changing the participant invalidates the fitted context; no model loads automatically. New validation is required after reload, and changed cap/reference conditions may require calibration again.
- **All-site quality is visible.** The current filtered window is checked across all 20 selected sites, with artifact/flat channel names and peak amplitudes available even for a custom montage without reference predictions. The scope continues to show C3/Cz/C4. Electrode spelling case no longer blanks otherwise valid scope channels. These checks are not impedance measurement or comprehensive artifact detection.
- **Discovery targets the selected EEG identity.** Three-class acquisition and preflight query the configured source ID/name directly and check for duplicates; unconfigured discovery collects available streams before checking uniqueness. This prevents an unrelated EEG outlet from ending a broad minimum-one query before the intended source appears. The real transport regression keeps an unrelated outlet active while selecting and then disconnecting the intended one. This follows the targeted-discovery interface in [pylsl](https://github.com/labstreaminglayer/pylsl).

The architecture remains the established 32-unit, three-member covariance neural ensemble. Four-second windows, .25-second stride, causal filtering and fixed confidence policy are retained. Every overlapping copy remains with its original cue. The GUI sends no actuator or FES commands.

The frozen binary app retains its earlier broad discovery behavior. For that lab validation, keep only the intended EEG outlet active; a multi-outlet binary revision is separate follow-up work. The three-class discovery fix does not alter the protected binary files or its ZIP.

## Model research performed alongside this revision

The [source-only low-rank study](MULTICLASS_V4_SOURCE_RESULTS.md) fitted four declared methods with three seeds across eight task/domain cases: 96 fits. It accessed training/tuning covariance rows only and computed no target-test predictions. The smaller full16 candidate gained just 0.56 percentage points on the mixed-case tuning mean, improving two cases and declining in six. PCA8/PCA16 lost useful information and underfit. That evidence does not support replacing the current lab architecture or claiming a new accuracy improvement.

The earlier 81.6% three-class cue balanced accuracy, 77.3% continuous replay cue score and 53.8% Volar Grasp recall retain their original recorded-participant scope. They do not predict a new wearer's result. Five/nine-class performance and cross-person transfer remain research work.

## Use and verification

Download the [revision-2 three-class ZIP](downloads/BCI_ThreeClass_Lab_2026-11-05_r2.zip), extract into its own folder, run its setup/launcher and follow [the three-class lab guide](THREECLASS_LAB_README.md). Keep the [binary ZIP](downloads/BCI_Binary_Lab_2026-11-05.zip) separate. Copy private model folders/reports separately if moving a previous personal calibration to a new extraction; recordings and personal weights are excluded from the public packages.

Software checks cover display acknowledgement, unseen transition gaps, chronology before quality exclusion, train-only preprocessing, rejected-cue scoring, explicit saved-model identity/hash checks, source-only covariance access, lower/upper-case physical labels and real LSL transport/disconnect handling. Synthetic integration tests check behavior, not EEG classification efficacy. The release's full test log and extracted-ZIP smoke checks are retained under `audit/`; actual X24/ESU/vendor integration and live personal accuracy remain for the lab.
